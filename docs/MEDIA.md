# Photography and place sources

Verified on **4 November 2026**. YangAtlas uses six real photographs downloaded from Wikimedia's own image CDN. No AI-generated image, stock placeholder, screenshot substitute, retouching, crop, recompression, or local format conversion was used. The files are Wikimedia's existing 1,280-pixel JPEG renditions, downloaded byte-for-byte. A browser may fit them within a card with CSS; this does not change the distributed files. The railway photograph's visible author watermark remains intact.

## What the images depict

- The lake photo depicts Hồ Xuân Hương; the station photo depicts Ga Đà Lạt. Those two cards can identify their actual subject.
- The garden card shows the actual flower park in **2007**. The Chùa Cầu card shows the actual bridge in **2020**, before its later restoration. Their visible captions retain those dates; neither image claims present-day conditions.
- The monastery, waterfall and café cards reuse the lake image as a **generic destination illustration**, not as photographs of those places.
- The street image illustrates Hội An old town. It is not claimed to show Tấn Ký house or Phúc Kiến assembly hall.
- The river image depicts Hội An at night. Its reuse on the An Bàng card is explicitly a **generic Hội An illustration**, not a beach photo.
- `places[].imageCaption`, `places[].photoId`, `destinations[].imageCaption` and `photoCredits` expose these distinctions to the interface. Do not replace these with misleading venue-specific alt text.

The photographs were taken in earlier years. They establish image provenance, not present conditions, venue opening status, weather, availability or service quality.

## Fictional planning data

**Every cost and duration in the catalog and sample trips is a fictional, editable example.** A zero amount means the example has no budget assigned; it is not a claim of free entry. In particular, the three Hội An heritage-site amounts are arbitrary budget allocations, not three separately sold tickets. Verify actual ticket arrangements directly before travel.

The two sample itineraries are product demonstration data, not a package tour or a recommendation validated on the ground. Meals, accommodation and transfers with no `placeId` are visitor-editable placeholders; no hotel, driver, restaurant reservation, transport ticket or availability is claimed. Start dates are replaceable example dates. The proposed visit order does not include computed travel time; leave room for travel and check local access conditions yourself.
